# Privacy Policy — Tulu Panchanga

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

Tulu Panchanga ("the app") is an offline calendar and panchanga for Tulunadu.
This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal
data.** It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does
not connect to the internet.

## Information on your device

The app saves a few settings only on your phone, so they are there next time
you open it:

- your chosen language and Tulu-lipi preference;
- your chosen place (a preset such as Mangaluru, or coordinates you type in);
- notification preferences;
- optional birth star / rashi for the muhurta helper.

These never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data
removes them.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — to show the optional morning panchanga, festival
  reminders and Rahu-kaala alerts you switch on.
- **Run at startup** — to set those reminders again after the phone restarts.

The app does **not** use your GPS location, contacts, camera, microphone,
files or internet connection.

## Read aloud

The "read aloud" button uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Any
processing is done by that engine under its own privacy terms; the app sends
nothing anywhere.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share**, the app creates an image and text of the day's
panchanga and hands it to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). Nothing
is shared unless you do this.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children.
